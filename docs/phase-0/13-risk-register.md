# Risk register

## Scoring and ownership

Likelihood (L) and impact (I) use 1 (low) to 5 (critical); score = L x I. Scores 15-25 are high, 8-14 medium, 1-7 low. Scores are planning aids, not substitutes for academic/security judgment.

Owner roles:

- **Department owner**: policy, academic consequences, staffing, risk acceptance.
- **Security owner**: threat/control acceptance, incident and privacy oversight.
- **Technical owner**: architecture, implementation, operations, recovery.
- **Exam operator**: configuration, readiness, candidate support, exam-day commands.

No developer silently accepts a high residual risk for a real examination.

## Phase 0 decisions requiring external approval

1. Conservative offline policy and proposed two-minute lease.
2. Production secure exams require SEB Config Key plus allowlisted Browser Exam Key where supported/tested.
3. Initial RPO <= 15 minutes and RTO <= 2 hours, with separate exam-day cancel/delay authority.
4. Proposed WCAG 2.2 AA target and accommodation governance.
5. Retention periods and access/appeal process for suspicious events, attempts, results, exports, backups, and audit.
6. Internal separation of administrator permissions and whether selected actions require two-person approval.

## Review process

Update likelihood, impact, mitigation evidence, owner, due phase, and residual status at every phase. A real exam release requires all high risks either demonstrably reduced or explicitly accepted in writing by the named Department and Security owners, with expiry/review dates.
